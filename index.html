Two AI employees, one runtime

Permissions, approval limits and data boundaries are enforced in code, outside the model.

D

Diya · Accounts Payable Associate

Reports to Meera Rao (Finance Manager)

  • May use: mail and the ERP only (anything else is refused in code)
  • Bills over ₹1,00,000 need the Finance Manager; over ₹5,00,000 the CFO
  • Any vendor bank-detail change needs approval; never deletes records
  • Her autonomy level (trust ladder) comes from her verified track record
K

Kabir · Recruiting Coordinator

Reports to Vikram Desai (Hiring Manager)

  • May use: mail, the job portal and the ATS
  • Screens applicants against the job description; records stages with reasons
  • Marks "Needs Info" instead of guessing missing data
  • Asked to do accounts work, he refuses and names the right colleague
